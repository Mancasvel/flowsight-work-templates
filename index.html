Work templates for clear reports and better reviews

A clear structure for the next decision. Download editable Markdown and CSV files for work reports, focus experiments and team conversations.

These resources are published by Manuel Castillejo, creator of FlowSight. Use them in your ordinary editor with or without the app. FlowSight summaries can supply drafting material; verifying outcomes and selecting the final information remain human decisions.

What a useful update contains

State what changed, show the evidence, identify what remains and ask for the next decision. Keep work in progress separate from completed outcomes.

Fictional example: guide reviewed in environment A; validation in environment B is pending; access is required before choosing a publication date.

Use a template proportionately

Choose the file that supports your actual purpose. A client handoff needs delivery state and authorized access; a team update needs decisions and dependencies; a focus trial needs a task and one adjustment. Remove fields that do not help the reader.

Compare any AI-generated material with project evidence. A recorded activity does not establish completion, and a timing pattern does not establish a cause. Include relevant work performed away from the device and state uncertainty when verification is incomplete.

Before sharing, inspect personal information, client names, internal references and the final recipient. Local analysis does not keep information private after a report is sent or processed by a connected service. Organizational use requires review of the actual purpose and processing.

The files provide a manual drafting structure. They do not promise automatic import, synchronized fields or capabilities absent from the installed FlowSight version. Check current product documentation for those capabilities.
